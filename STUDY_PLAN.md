# Study plan (10 weeks, ~1.5–2 h/day)

Built for a Staff-level Applied AI / ML engineer who already ships production RAG and agent systems. Your edge is GenAI
design and leadership stories. The biggest risk at FAANG is **coding speed and classic distributed systems depth**, so
coding gets the most hours early on.

**Every day (15 min):** `python tools/quiz.py --due` (or `quiz.html` on your phone).
**Every week:** 1 timed mock (alternate coding / system design / GenAI design / behavioral). Use a friend,
[interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io), [Hello Interview](https://www.hellointerview.com), or record yourself.

| Week | Focus | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategy + coding foundations | Read 00-strategy. Patterns 1–3 (arrays/strings, binary search, stacks/lists). 15 *must* problems. Draft stories 1–3 in [story-bank](06-behavioral/story-bank.md). |
| 2 | Coding: trees, graphs | Patterns 4–5. 15 problems. Do one session in a plain text editor with no running code (Google format). |
| 3 | Coding: heaps, backtracking, DP | Patterns 6–8. 15 problems. First timed mock: 2 mediums in 40 min (Meta format). |
| 4 | System design foundations | [framework](02-system-design/framework.md), [building blocks](02-system-design/building-blocks.md), [estimation](02-system-design/estimation.md). Case studies 1–4 out loud. 10 coding problems (re-solve misses). |
| 5 | System design depth | Case studies 5–12. Read DDIA ch. 5–9 (replication, partitioning, transactions, consistency). System design mock. |
| 6 | GenAI design I | [framework](04-genai-system-design/framework.md), [RAG](04-genai-system-design/rag.md), [evaluation](04-genai-system-design/evaluation.md). Rehearse case studies 1 and 3 (closest to your experience). Fill in stories 1–2 with real numbers. |
| 7 | GenAI design II | [Serving](04-genai-system-design/llm-serving.md), [agents](04-genai-system-design/agents.md), [safety](04-genai-system-design/safety-and-guardrails.md), [fine-tuning](04-genai-system-design/fine-tuning-and-alignment.md). Case studies 2, 4–8. GenAI design mock. Practice the [vendor-neutral mapping](04-genai-system-design/vendor-neutral-mapping.md) out loud. |
| 8 | ML design + fundamentals | [ML design framework](03-ml-system-design/framework.md), [metrics/A-B](03-ml-system-design/metrics-and-experimentation.md), case studies. [Transformers & LLMs](05-ml-fundamentals/transformers-and-llms.md): implement attention from scratch once. ML design mock. |
| 9 | Behavioral + AI-assisted coding | Finish all 10 stories; map them to the [question bank](06-behavioral/question-bank.md) and [Amazon LPs](06-behavioral/amazon-leadership-principles.md). Two [AI-assisted coding](00-strategy/ai-assisted-coding.md) drills. Behavioral mock. |
| 10 | Company-specific + polish | Company-tagged LeetCode for your target company (last 6 months). Re-do the hardest case studies. One full mock loop (4 rounds in a day). Rest the day before. |

## Compressed 4-week version

Week 1: coding patterns 1–8 (40 *must* problems). Week 2: system design + GenAI design. Week 3: ML design, fundamentals,
behavioral stories. Week 4: mocks every other day, company-specific practice, daily quiz review.
