## Company-flavored
- **Google (Googleyness & Leadership):** a time you challenged the status quo; a time you helped a colleague with no benefit to you;
  a time you had to work with someone very different from you; how you handle ambiguity.
- **Meta:** a time you moved fast and it broke something; your most impactful project measured by users; a time you
  pushed back on a product decision; how you grow in a role.
- **Microsoft:** growth mindset (a time you learned from failure); "One Microsoft" (collaboration across orgs); customer empathy.
- **Netflix:** a time you gave candid feedback to a peer; a decision you made with incomplete information and owned; how you'd
  handle the "keeper test".
- **Amazon:** see [amazon-leadership-principles.md](amazon-leadership-principles.md).
