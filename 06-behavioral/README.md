# Behavioral and leadership

At Staff level, behavioral rounds decide the **level** as often as the hire/no-hire. Meta's behavioral round can down-level on its
own; Amazon spends half of every interview on Leadership Principles; Google rates "Googleyness & Leadership" separately.

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [story-framework.md](story-framework.md) | STAR-L structure, Staff-level signals, how to answer follow-ups |
| [story-bank.md](story-bank.md) | **Your** story bank: skeletons built from your real projects, for you to fill in with specifics |
| [question-bank.md](question-bank.md) | ~60 common questions grouped by theme, mapped to stories |
| [amazon-leadership-principles.md](amazon-leadership-principles.md) | All 16 LPs, what interviewers listen for, sample questions |

**Rule of thumb:** prepare **8–10 strong stories**, each able to answer 3–5 different questions. Rehearse them out loud, out of
order, with a timer (2–3 min per story, then follow-ups).
