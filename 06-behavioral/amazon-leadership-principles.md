# Amazon Leadership Principles (all 16)

Source: [aboutamazon.com — Our Leadership Principles](https://www.aboutamazon.com/working-at-amazon/our-leadership-principles).
Paraphrased below; read the official wording before your loop.

Each interviewer is assigned 2–3 LPs and probes **2 stories each** with deep follow-ups. Prepare **two stories per LP**
(stories can repeat across LPs, but not within one loop with the same interviewer). Use metrics. Expect "Dive Deep"
follow-ups on any number you mention.

| # | Principle | What they listen for | Sample question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Customer Obsession** | Work backward from the customer; trade-offs in the customer's favor | Tell me about a time you used customer feedback to change a product. |
| 2 | **Ownership** | Acting beyond your role; long-term thinking; never "that's not my job" | Tell me about a time you took on something outside your responsibilities. |
| 3 | **Invent and Simplify** | New ideas; removing complexity; open to ideas from anywhere | Tell me about the simplest solution you've found to a complex problem. |
| 4 | **Are Right, A Lot** | Good judgment; seeking diverse views; disconfirming your own beliefs | Tell me about a decision you made where others disagreed and you were right (or wrong). |
| 5 | **Learn and Be Curious** | Continuous learning; exploring new things | Tell me about something you learned recently that you applied at work. |
| 6 | **Hire and Develop the Best** | Raising the bar in hiring; coaching others | Tell me about someone you developed and how. |
| 7 | **Insist on the Highest Standards** | High bar; fixing root causes; not shipping defects | Tell me about a time you refused to compromise on quality. |
| 8 | **Think Big** | Bold direction; communicating vision | Tell me about a time you proposed something much bigger than your mandate. |
| 9 | **Bias for Action** | Speed; calculated risk on reversible decisions | Tell me about a decision you made quickly with incomplete information. |
| 10 | **Frugality** | Doing more with less; resourcefulness | Tell me about a time you delivered with fewer resources than you wanted. |
| 11 | **Earn Trust** | Candor, self-criticism, respect; owning mistakes | Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news. |
| 12 | **Dive Deep** | Knowing the details; auditing metrics; finding root cause | Tell me about a time a metric looked fine but you dug in and found a problem. |
| 13 | **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** | Challenging respectfully with data; committing fully after the decision | Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager. What happened? |
| 14 | **Deliver Results** | Hitting key inputs/outputs despite setbacks | Tell me about a time you were about to miss a deadline. |
| 15 | **Strive to be Earth's Best Employer** | Creating a safe, inclusive, growth-oriented environment for your team | Tell me about a time you improved your team's working environment. |
| 16 | **Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility** | Considering second-order effects on people, communities, and the world | Tell me about a time you considered the broader impact of a technical decision (e.g. AI fairness, privacy). |

## Bar Raiser

An experienced interviewer from outside the hiring team whose job is to make sure every hire raises the bar. They can veto.
Their round often probes the **weakest-looking area** of your loop and uses heavier follow-ups. Stay specific and consistent.
